## Features Implemented

### Startup Information

* Startup Name
* Problem Statement
* Proposed Solution
* Revenue Model
* Target Audience
* Funding Ask

### AI Judges

* 🦈 Venture Capitalist – Evaluates market size and scalability.
* 🦈 Founder – Focuses on execution and product development.
* 🦈 Customer – Reviews usability and customer value.
* 🦈 Angel Investor – Assesses profitability and financial sustainability.

### Pitch Simulation

* Interactive startup pitch
* AI-generated investor questions
* User response input
* Dynamic AI judge reactions

### Startup Evaluation

* Market Potential Score
* Innovation Score
* Business Model Score
* Execution Score
* Investment Worthiness Score

### Investment Decision

* Invest
* Reject
* Acquire
* Come Back Later

Additional outputs:

* Suggested Startup Valuation
* Recommended Funding Amount
* Investment Reasoning

### User Interface

* Modern dark theme
* Shark Tank inspired design
* Responsive layout
* Animated cards
* Interactive dashboard

### Bonus Features

* Confetti animation on successful investment
* Download Pitch Report (PDF)
* Leaderboard
* Share Result button

## Key Learnings

* Investor evaluation considers market potential, execution capability, profitability, and scalability together.
* A compelling startup pitch must clearly define the problem, solution, and business model.
* Interactive simulations provide valuable practice for responding to investor questions.
* Well-designed dashboards improve presentation and understanding of business metrics.
* A polished user interface enhances the overall user experience and engagement.
