# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective

Today I used AI to create a Customer & MVP Blueprint for my startup idea. The objective was to identify the ideal customer, understand their pain points, map their journey, and define the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) required for validation.

---

## Startup Overview

### Startup Idea

AI Career Copilot – An AI-powered platform that helps students and fresh graduates with resume building, interview preparation, skill gap analysis, learning roadmaps, and job application tracking.

### Problem Statement

Students often struggle with career planning, resume optimization, interview preparation, and finding the right learning path. Existing solutions are fragmented across multiple platforms and require significant effort to manage.

### Target Customers

* College Students
* Final-Year Students
* Fresh Graduates
* Internship Seekers
* Early Career Professionals

## Top 10 Customer Pain Points

1. Lack of personalized career guidance.
2. Poor resume quality and ATS compatibility.
3. Interview anxiety and lack of preparation.
4. Difficulty identifying relevant skills.
5. Scattered learning resources.
6. Tracking multiple job applications manually.
7. Lack of mentorship.
8. Confusion about career paths.
9. Low awareness of industry expectations.
10. Difficulty finding suitable internships.

---

## Customer Journey

### Awareness

* Discovers platform through social media, college events, or referrals.
* Searches for resume and interview help online.

### Consideration

* Compares available career guidance platforms.
* Tests free tools and assessments.

### Purchase

* Subscribes to premium career assistance features.
* Uses AI-powered recommendations and coaching.

### Retention

* Continues using interview prep, application tracking, and learning roadmaps.
* Refers friends and classmates.

---

## Key Customer Objections

| Objection               | Response                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Too expensive           | Offer student pricing                |
| AI may not be accurate  | Provide expert-reviewed guidance     |
| Free alternatives exist | Deliver personalized recommendations |
| Data privacy concerns   | Maintain transparent policies        |
| Lack of trust           | Showcase testimonials and results    |

## MVP Recommendation

### What Should Be Built First

* Resume Analyzer
* ATS Score Checker
* Interview Question Generator
* Skill Gap Analysis
* Career Roadmap Generator

### What Should NOT Be Built Initially

* Community Features
* Social Networking
* Advanced Analytics
* Enterprise Dashboard
* Multi-language Support

### Success Metrics

* User Signups
* Daily Active Users
* Resume Analyses Completed
* Interview Sessions Generated
* User Retention Rate

## Final Verdict

### 🟡 Promising but Unvalidated

The problem is real and frequently experienced by students and fresh graduates. The market is large and accessible, but additional customer interviews and MVP testing are required before confirming strong product-market fit.

Recommended next step: Build and validate a focused MVP with real users before expanding feature scope.

---

## Key Learnings

* Understanding customer pain points is essential before building products.
* A focused MVP reduces development risk.
* Customer interviews are more valuable than assumptions.
* Prioritization frameworks help avoid feature overload.
* Early validation improves startup success probability.
