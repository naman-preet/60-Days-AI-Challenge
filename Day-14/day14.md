# 🚀 Day 14/60 - Job Red Flag Detector

## Objective

Today I learned how to evaluate job opportunities beyond salary and job titles by identifying potential red flags in job descriptions and company information. Using Claude AI, I analyzed a Data Analyst job posting and assessed risks related to company culture, hiring practices, job requirements, and workplace expectations.

---

## Prompt Used

The Job Red Flag Detector prompt was used to analyze:

* Job Description
* Company Information
* Hiring Risks
* Workplace Culture
* Company Stability
* Remote Work Authenticity

The objective was to determine whether the opportunity was worth applying for and identify any warning signs before proceeding with the hiring process.

## Detailed Assessment

### Requirements Risk

**Low Risk**

The company requires common Data Analyst skills such as SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, and Statistics. Experience requirements are appropriate for freshers and early-career professionals.

### Culture Risk

**Medium Risk**

Keywords such as:

* Fast-paced environment
* Multiple projects
* High delivery expectations

may indicate periods of increased workload. Candidates should clarify work-life balance during interviews.

### Remote/Hybrid Risk

**Low Risk**

The company clearly specifies a hybrid work model with no misleading remote claims.

### Hiring Risk

**Low Risk**

Positive signs include:

* Salary transparency
* Clearly defined responsibilities
* Realistic qualification requirements

### Company Risk

**Low Risk**

The organization demonstrates growth, employee investment, and market demand for analytics services.

---

## Final Verdict

### ⚠️ Apply with Caution

The opportunity appears legitimate and suitable for aspiring Data Analysts. However, candidates should ask questions regarding workload, deadlines, and expectations during busy project cycles before accepting an offer.

---

## 5 Smart Interview Questions

### 1. How is workload distributed when multiple projects are running simultaneously?

This helps assess work-life balance and team support.

### 2. What does success look like for a Data Analyst during the first six months?

Helps clarify performance expectations.

### 3. How often do employees need to provide support outside regular working hours?

Validates the weekend support requirement.

### 4. What training or certification opportunities are available for analysts?

Confirms professional development support.

### 5. How is collaboration managed between analysts and business stakeholders?

Provides insight into communication and teamwork expectations.

## Key Learnings

1. A high salary does not always indicate a good opportunity.
2. Job descriptions often reveal hidden workplace expectations.
3. Salary transparency is a positive hiring signal.
4. Work-life balance indicators should be carefully evaluated.
5. Company growth and employee development programs reduce risk.
6. Asking targeted interview questions helps validate employer claims.
7. AI can quickly identify potential job risks before applying.

---

## Conclusion

This exercise demonstrated how AI can help job seekers make informed career decisions. By analyzing job requirements, company information, hiring practices, and workplace culture, I was able to identify both opportunities and risks associated with a Data Analyst role. The analysis highlighted the importance of evaluating companies carefully before applying or accepting an offer.
