# Day 27 – Prior Authorization Story Simulator

## Objective

Develop a single-file interactive **Prior Authorization Story Simulator** that teaches the U.S. healthcare Prior Authorization (PA) process through Rahul's journey. The application uses an engaging story format, interactive choices, and educational explanations to help users understand how prior authorization works from both the patient and healthcare system perspectives.

## Features Implemented

### Story Characters

* 👦 Rahul – Patient
* 👧 Priya – Healthcare Operations Specialist
* 👨‍⚕️ Doctor and Narrator displayed as centered italic text

### Story Chapters

1. Doctor Visit
2. Insurance Roadblock
3. What is Prior Authorization?
4. Insurance Review
5. Denial
6. Appeal
7. Approval

* Append-only chat conversation
* Two user choices after every scene

## Key Learnings

* Prior Authorization helps insurance companies verify that treatments are medically necessary before approving coverage.
* Missing clinical documentation or step therapy records can delay approval but does not always mean permanent denial.
* The appeal process allows providers to submit additional evidence to support medical necessity.
* Interactive storytelling makes complex healthcare workflows easier to understand.
* Well-designed educational applications can simplify technical healthcare concepts for beginners.
