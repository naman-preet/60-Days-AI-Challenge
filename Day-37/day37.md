# Day 37 – Task Compass

## Objective
Built an interactive Task Compass application to understand how work flows through organizations using a game-based simulation.

## Features
- Offline single-file HTML application
- Three interactive learning stages
- Drag-and-drop role assignments
- Task routing simulation
- Collaboration challenges
- Progress tracking
- Reflection report
- Responsive dark glassmorphism UI

## Stages Completed
1. Who Owns This?
2. Task Routing
3. Collaboration Challenge

## What I Learned
- Ownership should be assigned based on responsibility, not job title alone.
- Effective delegation keeps work moving efficiently.
- Many workplace challenges require collaboration across multiple roles.
- Understanding workflow improves team coordination.

## Key Takeaways
- Clear ownership prevents confusion.
- Delegation and escalation are essential for complex tasks.
- Collaboration often leads to better outcomes than individual effort.
- Organizational workflows involve multiple stakeholders.

## Outcome
Successfully generated, tested, and completed the Task Compass application, gaining insights into organizational workflows and teamwork.
