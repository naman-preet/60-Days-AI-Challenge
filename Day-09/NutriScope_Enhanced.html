RDI Calculation Method

Calorie targets use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation adjusted by the selected activity multiplier (PAL). Macronutrient splits follow AMDR guidelines: protein 10–35%, fat 20–35%, carbohydrate 45–65% of total calories. Micronutrient RDIs are derived from the NIH Dietary Reference Intakes (DRIs), with values varying by age and sex as reflected in your profile.

Limitations & Disclaimer
  • Nutritional values are per 100 g raw weight unless otherwise stated.
  • Cooking methods significantly alter nutrient profiles (e.g. boiling reduces water-soluble vitamins).
  • Bioavailability varies; listed values represent total content, not absorbed quantity.
  • This tool does not account for drug–nutrient interactions or medical conditions.
  • Data is periodically updated; slight differences from other sources may occur.
