# Day 19 - Football Intelligence Hub

## Objective

The objective of Day 19 was to explore AI-powered sports intelligence using Claude and a football workbook. The exercise combined football analytics, prediction modeling, knowledge assessment, and personality analysis to create a personalized Football Intelligence Profile.

---

## Overview

The Football Intelligence Hub consisted of three major stages:

### Stage 0 – Knowledge Level Check

Assessed my familiarity with football to customize explanations and quiz difficulty.

### Stage 1 – FIFA World Cup 2026 Prediction Report

Generated predictions based on workbook data, historical trends, team strength, and player performance.

### Stage 2 – Football IQ Quiz

Evaluated football knowledge and awareness through multiple-choice questions.

### Stage 3 – Messi vs Ronaldo Personality Match

Analyzed personality traits and matched them with football legends and archetypes.
