# Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer

## Objective
Built and explored an interactive Cognitive Pattern Explorer web application that encourages self-reflection through engaging scenarios and activities.

## Features
- Offline single-file HTML application
- Calm and Stress modes
- Interactive scenario-based exploration
- Drag-and-drop priority cards
- Thinking timeline arrangement
- Personalized Reflection Journal
- Progress tracking
- Responsive and accessible design

## Chapters Completed
1. Discover Your Thinking Style
2. Choose Your Priorities
3. Map Your Thinking

## What I Learned
- Different situations can influence decision-making.
- Reflective thinking benefits from considering multiple perspectives.
- Interactive exercises make self-reflection more engaging.
- Organizing priorities helps reveal thinking tendencies.

## Key Takeaways
- The application promotes reflection rather than evaluation.
- Calm and Stress modes provide different experiences.
- Personalized feedback encourages thoughtful self-assessment.

## Outcome
Successfully generated, tested, and explored the Cognitive Pattern Explorer application in both modes, reviewed the Reflection Journal, and documented key learnings.
