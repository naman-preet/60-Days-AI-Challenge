# Day 26 – Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator

## Objective

Develop a single-file, interactive **Prior Authorization Workflow Simulator** that demonstrates the U.S. healthcare Prior Authorization (PA) process through a gamified drag-and-drop experience. The simulator helps users understand how patients, providers, and payers interact during the authorization process.

## Key Learnings

* Prior Authorization is a structured process involving coordination between patients, providers, and insurance payers.
* Proper documentation is essential for faster approval decisions.
* Different review outcomes, such as approval, denial, or appeal, depend on medical necessity and submitted evidence.
* Interactive simulations make complex healthcare workflows easier to understand.
* Responsive UI and drag-and-drop interactions improve learning and user engagement.
