# Day 16 - Custom Skill: Stock Fundamental Research

## Objective

The objective of Day 16 was to learn how to create and use a Custom Skill in Claude for conducting structured stock fundamental analysis. The skill was designed to analyze listed companies using financial statements, valuation metrics, business quality, growth prospects, ownership patterns, and risk factors without providing investment recommendations.

---

## Skill Details

### Skill Name

**stock-fundamental-research**

### Description

Analyze Indian and global listed companies using fundamentals, financial statements, business quality, competitive advantages, valuation, risks, and growth prospects. Generate evidence-based research reports and investor-friendly summaries. Never provide direct buy, sell, or hold recommendations.

---

## Steps Performed

1. Opened Claude.
2. Set the effort level to Low/Medium.
3. Navigated to the Skills section.
4. Created a new Custom Skill.
5. Named the skill **stock-fundamental-research**.
6. Added the provided description.
7. Pasted the complete instruction set.
8. Saved the skill successfully.
9. Tested the skill using TCS and Infosys.
10. Generated stock research reports and comparison reports.
11. Reviewed valuation metrics, financial health, ownership patterns, and risk factors.
12. Captured screenshots of the skill configuration and generated reports.
13. Created the Day16 folder in GitHub.
14. Uploaded screenshots and documentation.
15. Committed and pushed the changes.

---

## Sample Prompt Used

### Quick Take

Analyze Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) using Quick Take mode.

### Deep Dive

Analyze Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) using Deep Dive mode. Include valuation analysis, growth trends, ownership structure, peer comparison, financial health, and risk factors.

### Compare

Compare TCS and Infosys using Compare mode. Include valuation metrics, growth trends, profitability, ownership patterns, and stock price charts.

---

## Key Features of the Skill

* Reusable without re-entering long prompts.
* Generates structured stock research reports.
* Covers financial, business, and valuation analysis.
* Includes peer comparison.
* Identifies strengths and risks.
* Provides investor-friendly summaries.
* Avoids investment advice and target prices.

---

## Key Learnings

### 1. Custom Skills Improve Productivity

Creating a custom skill allows repeated execution of a specialized workflow without rewriting instructions.

### 2. Importance of Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis helps evaluate a company’s financial performance, business quality, profitability, and growth prospects.

### 3. Financial Metrics Matter

Key metrics such as P/E Ratio, ROE, ROCE, Debt-to-Equity Ratio, Revenue Growth, and Profit Growth provide valuable insights into company performance.

### 4. Risk Assessment is Essential

A good stock analysis includes both strengths and potential risks instead of focusing only on positive factors.

### 5. Structured Outputs Improve Consistency

The skill generated reports in a consistent format, making it easier to compare multiple companies.
